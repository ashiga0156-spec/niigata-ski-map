About this map

The Niigata Ski & Sake Map is a free, mobile-friendly map for visitors planning a ski trip to the Yuzawa and Myoko areas of Niigata, Japan. It shows ski resorts, sake breweries and key transport hubs, with short notes in English and links to help you plan.

It is part of the Niigata Umami Guide, an English-language publication about Niigata food, sake and travel.

How to use it

  • Tap a pin to see a short description and useful links.
  • Use the Ski, Sake and Access buttons to filter the map.
  • Add the map to your phone's home screen to open it like an app. Places and map areas you have viewed stay available offline.

Accuracy

Map data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors. Opening hours, prices, tour availability and winter conditions change often. Please check the official website of each place before you travel. Pin positions are approximate and may be adjusted over time.
